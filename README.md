## English

### 📋 Overview

An **interactive web-based practice test** to help prepare for the French Civic Exam required for:
- **CSP (Carte de Séjour Pluriannuelle)** — Multi-year residence permit
- **CR (Carte de Résident)** — Permanent residence card

This free tool provides a realistic exam experience with instant feedback, progress tracking, and comprehensive coverage of all official topics.

***

### ✨ Features

- ✅ **Two exam types**: CSP and CR with specific question banks
- ✅ **130+ authentic questions** covering all official topics
- ✅ **Randomized selection**: 40 questions per test
- ✅ **Instant feedback**: Know immediately if your answer is correct
- ✅ **Progress tracking**: Visual progress bar and question counter
- ✅ **Pass/Fail scoring**: 80% passing threshold (official requirement)
- ✅ **Mobile-friendly**: Works perfectly on smartphones and tablets
- ✅ **No installation**: Works directly in your browser
- ✅ **Offline capable**: Save the page and use without internet
- ✅ **Free & Open Source**: No ads, no registration required

***

### 🎯 How to Use

#### **Option 1: Use the Live Demo**

1. **Visit the live test**: [https://your-username.github.io/your-repo-name/](https://your-username.github.io/your-repo-name/)
2. **Select your exam type** from the dropdown:
   - **CSP** for multi-year residence permit
   - **CR** for permanent residence card
3. **Click on an answer** to select it
4. **Review feedback** (green = correct, red = incorrect)
5. **Click "Suivant"** to move to the next question
6. **Complete all 40 questions** to see your final score

#### **Option 2: Use on Mobile (iPhone/Android)**

1. **Download the HTML file** from this repository

3. **Works without internet** — perfect for studying on the go
